About me

My background

I was born in Liberia and moved to the United States before starting high school. I am now studying computer science at UC San Diego

I enjoy building things that connect a clear user need with practical technical work. My current learning includes web development, operating systems, and data science.

What I am developing

  • Website structure: organizing information into useful pages with semantic HTML.
  • Data tools: presenting statistics and making their assumptions easier to inspect.
  • Iteration: using feedback and verification to identify what needs to change.

My case studies describe actual work and its limits. I am still developing my skills and will discuss scope honestly.

Working together

  1. Describe the people who will use the site and what they need to do.
  2. Agree on the content, pages, priorities, and a realistic first version.
  3. Review a small working version and identify confusing or missing parts.
  4. Check the result and discuss the next steps.

This is my proposed process for a future project, not a claim of past client experience. A smaller, clear scope is easier to review and improve.
