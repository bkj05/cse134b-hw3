MathML extra credit

Einstein's mass-energy equivalence relates energy, mass, and the speed of light:

E=mc2

In plain text: E = m times c squared. E is energy, m is mass, and c is the speed of light.

Being part of a standard does not make a feature reliably available in every browser. MathML support was uneven for years: Firefox supported it, while many Chrome and Edge versions did not enable it by default. Support in Chrome and Edge from version 109 made native MathML more practical across major browsers.

Check the MathML browser-support table (checked October 13, 2026).
